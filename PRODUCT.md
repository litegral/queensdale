# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site, deployed to Cloudflare Workers or Pages.

## Users

Friends invited to a small private Minecraft world who need the server details, connection address, and first-time login instructions.

## Product Purpose

Queensdale gives a group of friends a shared, friendly survival world. The website's job is to make joining simple without presenting the world as a public or commercial server.

## Positioning

A little world of our own: friendly survival, mostly vanilla, and intentionally made for friends.

## Operating Context

Players connect with Minecraft Java Edition 26.1.2, copy the address `mc.litegral.com`, register once with `/register <password>`, and later return with `/login <password>`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Survival on easy difficulty
- Mostly vanilla gameplay
- Land claims and protected builds
- Premium and offline accounts supported
- 50% sleep requirement
- Performance-optimized Fabric server
- Optional Simple Voice Chat; proximity voice requires the client mod
- No additional whitelist step is presented on the site

## Brand Commitments

The name is Queensdale. The voice is personal, calm, understated, and direct, never commercial or promotional. The interface uses DM Sans, neutral whites, warm grays, charcoal text, and excellent dark mode. It avoids gamer aesthetics, gradients, neon colors, pixel fonts, oversized cards, heavy shadows, and flashy animation.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied server configuration, address, authentication commands, and rules are the source of truth. There are no testimonials, player counts, screenshots, or commercial claims to present.

## Product Principles

- Make the server address and joining action immediately clear.
- Feel like a private note between friends, not a public server advertisement.
- Keep the entire experience compact, quiet, and easy to scan.
- State optional setup and community expectations plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain accessible contrast, responsive mobile-first layout, visible keyboard focus, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, and an understandable copied-state announcement.
